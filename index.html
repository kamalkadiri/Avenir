/model · for investors

Fees on every turn. Data from every asset.

Spreads compress as a market matures. Origination fees and asset data compound.

  1. 01 fund

    Warehouse line behind first-loss equity.

  2. 02 lend

    Receivables advances and equipment leases.

  3. 03 record

    Uptime, collections and resale data.

  4. 04 reprice

    Higher advance rates, lower losses.

  5. 05 sell

    Seasoned loans to credit funds.
